The '''2026 Campeonato Brasileiro Série A''' is the 70th season of the [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série A]], the top level of professional football in Brazil, and the 24th edition in a double round-robin since its establishment in 2003. The competition began on 28 January and will end on 2 December 2026,<ref name=GE>{{cite web|url=https://ge.globo.com/rj/futebol/noticia/2025/10/01/brasileirao-2026-sera-disputado-de-janeiro-a-dezembro-entenda-a-mudanca.ghtml|title=Brasileirão 2026 será disputado de janeiro a dezembro: entenda a mudança|trans-title=2026 ''Brasileirão'' will be played from January to December: understand the changes|publisher=[[Grupo Globo|ge]]|language=pt-BR|date=1 October 2025|accessdate=2 December 2025}}</ref> including a month-and-a-half long break for the [[2026 FIFA World Cup]].

The top five teams as well as the [[2026 Copa do Brasil]] champions and runner-up will qualify for the [[Copa Libertadores|2027 Copa Libertadores]], a change from previous years when the top six teams and only the Copa do Brasil champions qualified.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-12-15 |title=Vice da Copa do Brasil 2026 terá vaga na fase preliminar da Libertadores |url=https://www.uol.com.br/esporte/futebol/ultimas-noticias/2025/12/15/vice-da-copa-do-brasil-2026-tera-vaga-na-fase-preliminar-da-libertadores.htm |access-date=2025-12-16 |website=UOL |language=pt-br}}</ref> Meanwhile, the next six best-placed teams not qualified for Copa Libertadores will qualify for the [[Copa Sudamericana|2027 Copa Sudamericana]], and the last four will be relegated to [[Campeonato Brasileiro Série B|Série B]] for 2027.

[[CR Flamengo|Flamengo]] are the defending champions, having won their eighth title in the previous season. Officially named '''Brasileirão [[Betano (company)|Betano]] 2026''' for sponsorship reasons, the sponsorship ended due to the enactment of the [[Provisional Measure 1394/2026]] in September.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.terra.com.br/esportes/futebol/copa-do-brasil/perfis-do-brasileirao-e-copa-do-brasil-tiram-naming-rights-de-bets-nas-redes-sociais,5efae5b9e19b2614da580be34cffcf96au2quvl6.html|title=Perfis do Brasileirão e Copa do Brasil tiram naming rights de bets nas redes sociais|trans-title=''Brasileirão'' and Copa do Brasil social media accounts remove betting company naming rights|publisher=[[Terra (company)|Terra]]|language=pt-BR|date=1 October 2026|accessdate=6 October 2026}}</ref>

==Teams==
